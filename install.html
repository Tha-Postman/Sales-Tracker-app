Install Sales Tracker

Use Sales Tracker from your desktop or iPhone home screen.

Choose the option that fits your device. Your account, products, reps, sales, and reports stay synced through the same secure backend.

Windows desktop

Download the desktop app

Download the Windows package, extract it, then open Sales Tracker.exe. You can pin it to your taskbar or create a desktop shortcut.

  1. Click download.
  2. Extract the downloaded zip file.
  3. Open Sales Tracker.exe.
  4. Sign in with your Sales Tracker account.
Download Windows App

If the download is not available yet, open support chat and request the latest Windows build.

iPhone and iPad

Add to Home Screen

Install the website like an app from Safari. This gives you a Sales Tracker icon on your iPhone without waiting for App Store publishing.

  1. Open Sales Tracker in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add, then open the new icon.
