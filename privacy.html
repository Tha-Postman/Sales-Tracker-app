Sales Tracker

Privacy Policy

We collect account, business, product, sales, team, and payment-related data needed to operate Sales Tracker.

How data is used

Data is used to authenticate users, activate subscriptions, process sales records, generate reports, and support businesses.

Payment data

Card payments are processed by Paystack. Sales Tracker does not store card numbers.

Security

We use role-based access, business-level data isolation, and server-side subscription checks.
